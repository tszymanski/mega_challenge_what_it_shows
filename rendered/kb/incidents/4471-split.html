The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Day-first tenants get day and month swapped: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” Every tenant on file, every channel. This is the fix #4471 had queued; what shipped covers EU locales only.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Same swap, different bug: only reports through the form, only tenants on booking-v3. Fixing the parser does not touch it; the hint has to follow the tenant’s locale.

one-day: the date moves by exactly one day. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” No month or day is swapped, so neither fix above reaches it. It reads like a timezone conversion on the date. The desk already noted these (desk-notes.json) and files them under parser by tenant, so they count in both rows.
